Home Brain

Home Brain is a private app used only by one family. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy policy

With each family member's permission, Home Brain reads that person's own Gmail, Google Calendar, Contacts and Drive to help them organise their day. The data is processed on the family's own home server. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Each person can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
